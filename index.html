ABOUT THE EVENT

MORE THAN A MATCH.

KH Esports Championship 2026 is a competitive gaming event organised by Techverse Club, SMJK Keat Hwa.

The championship brings together competitive players in two major titles: Honor of Kings and Mobile Legends: Bang Bang.

Beyond competition, the event aims to create a structured and professional esports environment where teamwork, communication, discipline, strategy and sportsmanship are equally valued.

8 groups × 4 teams. Every team faces every other team in its group once.

COMPETITION RULES

PLAY FAIR. PLAY HARD.

01

GROUP STAGE

32 teams are divided into 8 groups, with 4 teams in each group.

02

ROUND ROBIN

Teams compete against every other team within their group once.

03

ADVANCEMENT

The top two teams from each group advance to the knockout stage.

04

KNOCKOUT

The knockout stage uses a single-elimination structure.

05

SERIES FORMAT

Quarterfinals and Semifinals are BO3. Grand Final is BO5.

06

FAIR PLAY

All teams must follow the official tournament rules and organiser decisions.
