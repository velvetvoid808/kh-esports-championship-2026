KH Esports Championship 2026 is a competitive esports event organised by Techverse Club of SMJK Keat Hwa.

GROUP STAGE

64 teams are divided into 16 groups of 4 teams. Each group competes in a round-robin format.

KNOCKOUT STAGE

Group winners advance to the Round of 16 before progressing through the knockout bracket.

GRAND FINAL

The final two teams face off in a Best-of-5 series for the championship title.
