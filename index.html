ABOUT KH ESPORTS

Keat Hwa Esports Championship 2026 is a competitive esports platform designed to provide students with an opportunity to develop their strategic thinking, communication, leadership, teamwork and sportsmanship.

Organised by Techverse Club SMJK Keat Hwa, the championship brings together two competitive titles in a structured and professional tournament environment.

KEY RULES

01

GLOBAL BAN & PICK

Each team may only select the same Hero once throughout a BO3 or BO5 series.

02

TIE-BREAK

Head-to-Head will be used first. If it cannot determine the ranking, a tie-break match or draw may be used.

03

WALKOVER

Teams must be present at least 15 minutes before their scheduled match.

04

FAIR PLAY

Cheating, abusive behaviour, disruption or other violations may result in disciplinary action.
