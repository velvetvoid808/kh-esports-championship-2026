ABOUT KH ESPORTS

Keat Hwa Esports Championship 2026 is a competitive esports platform designed to provide students with an opportunity to develop their strategic thinking, communication, leadership, teamwork and sportsmanship.

Organised by Techverse Club SMJK Keat Hwa, the championship brings together two competitive titles in a structured and professional tournament environment.
